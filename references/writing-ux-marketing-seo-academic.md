# Indonesian Content Writing: Registers, Marketing, Web Copy, SEO & Academia

Beyond software localization (`i18n`), `indonesian-linguist` governs long-form and short-form Indonesian writing across five registers and four specialized mediums.

---

## 1. Ivan Lanin's Five Registers (*Lima Laras Bahasa*)

Before writing a single word, match the register to the relationship between writer, reader, and medium:

| Register | Characteristics | Pronouns | Typical Mediums |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Beku** *(Frozen)* | Unchanging, ceremonial, statutory | Third-person titles (*Pihak Pertama, Pemohon*) | Laws, contracts, oaths, certificates |
| **2. Resmi** *(Formal)* | Complete EYD V syntax, zero slang, objective | *Anda*, *Bapak/Ibu*, *kami*, passive voice | Academic papers, official reports, press releases, government/banking notices |
| **3. Konsultatif** *(Consultative)* | Standard vocabulary, crisp active verbs, approachable | *Anda* (B2B/general) or *kamu* (modern tech) | Documentation, news articles, SEO guides, work emails, B2B landing pages |
| **4. Santai** *(Casual)* | Conversational rhythm, relaxed particles (*kok, sih, lho, nggak*), still spelled correctly | *kamu / -mu*, *kita* | Consumer app copy, social media captions, personal blogs, newsletters |
| **5. Akrab** *(Intimate)* | High context, elliptical sentences, community slang | *kamu / -mu*, *gue / lo* (if brand persona fits) | Community DMs, gym-buddy nudges, peer group chats |

## 2. Marketing & Social Media Copywriting

1. **Hook Specificity (First 80 Characters)**:
   - On Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, and X, only the first 1-2 lines appear before *"selengkapnya"*.
   - Open with a concrete scene, friction point, or contrarian number, never a generic announcement (*"Kabar gembira buat kamu semua!"*).
   - *Example*:
     - ❌ *"Buat kamu pemilik warung, ini kabar yang kamu tunggu-tunggu! Usaha lancar, cuan utuh!"*
     - ✅ **"Ada pembeli di warungmu pegang HP, bukan dompet. 'Bisa bayar QRIS?' Sekarang jawabannya gampang: bisa."**
2. **Indonesia Advertising Ethics (*Etika Pariwara Indonesia / EPI*)**:
   - Never use absolute superlatives (*terbaik, nomor 1, paling murah, satu-satunya, 100% pasti*) without citing a verifiable, dated third-party source.
3. **Single Call-to-Action (CTA)**:
   - End with **one** concrete action (*"Buka menu Pengaturan lalu aktifkan QRIS sekarang"*), not three competing asks (*"Like, comment, share, dan klik link di bio ya!"*).
4. **Hashtag & Emoji Discipline**:
   - Maximum 3-5 lowercase relevant hashtags at the very bottom when requested. Never use emojis as bullet points on every line.

## 3. Website & Landing Page Copy

1. **Hero Section Formula**:
   - **Headline (±10 words)**: State who it is for and the concrete outcome (*"Pelacak latihan beban tanpa iklan. Catat set, repetisi, dan PR dalam hitungan detik"*), never empty corporate slogans (*"Solusi kebugaran digital masa depan Anda"*).
   - **Subheadline**: Explain how it works and remove the primary objection (e.g., *"Gratis, bisa berjalan offline di gym bawah tanah, dan datamu tetap milikmu"*).
2. **Proof Near Claims**:
   - Place numbers, concrete examples, or user quotes immediately adjacent to the feature claim.
3. **About Us & FAQ**:
   - *Tentang Kami*: Tell the specific origin story, facts, and principles, ban *"Kami berkomitmen memberikan pelayanan terbaik dengan mengedepankan profesionalisme"*.
   - *FAQ*: Phrase questions in the exact words real users ask, and answer the question in the very first sentence (*Ya / Tidak / Bisa, caranya...*).

## 4. Indonesian SEO & Long-Form Articles

1. **Title Tag (`<title>`) & H1**:
   - Keep `title` under **60 characters** so Google SERPs do not truncate it.
   - Front-load the primary Indonesian search query naturally.
   - Use **Sentence case** (capitalize only the first word and proper nouns), which reads more authentically than English-style Title Case.
   - *Example*: ✅ **`Cara mengatur jadwal latihan push pull legs 3 hari seminggu`** (59 chars).
2. **Meta Description**:
   - 120-155 characters summarizing the concrete answer or takeaway inside the article.
3. **Answer-First Structure (*Helpful Content*)**:
   - Answer the reader's core query in the first paragraph. Do not bury a simple answer beneath 300 words of *"Pengertian X menurut para ahli"*.
   - Use natural semantic variations rather than repeating an awkward keyword string (*"jual sepatu lari murah jakarta"*) verbatim in every paragraph.

## 5. Academic & Research Writing (*Karya Ilmiah / Skripsi / Jurnal*)

1. **Strict Register & Objectivity**:
   - Use *Ragam Resmi* (EYD V). Avoid first-person singular *saya / aku*. Use **`penulis`**, **`peneliti`**, or passive constructions (*"Data dianalisis menggunakan..."*).
2. **Zero Fabricated Citations (Absolute Rule)**:
   - **NEVER** invent author names, publication years, DOIs, or journal titles. If a claim needs a citation that is not in the provided context or verified via search tools, insert `[perlu rujukan]` and inform the user.
3. **IMRaD Precision**:
   - *Abstrak*: 150-250 words in a single paragraph containing background (1 sentence), objective, method, quantitative key findings, and implication.
   - *Tinjauan Pustaka*: Synthesize and compare prior studies (*"Sejalan dengan temuan A (2024), tetapi berbeda pada variabel..."*) rather than listing disconnected summaries paragraph by paragraph (*"Penelitian A (2022) menyatakan... Penelitian B (2023) menyatakan..."*).
